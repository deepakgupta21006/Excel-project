# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

# Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from FNP (Ferns N Petals), a gifting company, to evaluate revenue trends,customer spending, product performance,
and order delivery efficiency. An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and
support data-driven decision-making.

# Business Objective

The objective is to analyze FNP's sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing products, customer purchasing patterns, popular gifting occasions, 
and delivery performance. The analysis helps identify opportunities to improve sales strategy, inventory planning, and customer satisfaction.

# Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Dashboard development and data analysis

- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

- ETL – Extracting, transforming, and preparing data for analysis

- Data Modeling – Organizing data and establishing relationships

- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts – Summarizing data and visualizing trends

- DAX – Creating calculated measures and KPIs, where applicable

# Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive visualizations to analyze:

1. Monthly Sales Performance – Compares revenue across months.

2. Top 5 Products by Revenue – Identifies leading revenue-generating products.

3. Revenue by Order Hour – Examines revenue patterns by order time.

4. Revenue by Product Category – Compares category-level performance.

5. Top 10 Cities by Orders – Highlights cities with the highest order counts.

6. Revenue by Occasion – Compares revenue across gifting occasions.

7. Interactive Filters – Enables analysis by order date, delivery date, and occasion.

# Key Business Insights

- Revenue: The business generated approximately ₹35.21 lakh across 1,000 orders.

- Monthly Performance: August recorded the highest monthly revenue in the analysis.

- Product Categories: Colors was the highest revenue-generating category.

- Gifting Occasions: Anniversary generated the highest revenue among the displayed occasions.

- Customer Spending: Average spending per order was approximately ₹3,521.

- Delivery Efficiency: The average order-to-delivery time was 5.53 days.

# Business Recommendations

- Plan inventory according to high-performing products and categories.

- Develop targeted marketing campaigns around popular gifting occasions.

- Investigate monthly sales fluctuations to identify growth opportunities.

- Analyze city-level order patterns to improve regional marketing strategies.

- Review delivery timelines to identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.
